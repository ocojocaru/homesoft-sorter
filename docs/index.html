Questions

Does it upload my files or scan them online?

No. Sorting happens entirely on your computer. The app only contacts the internet to activate or re-check a license, to protect the free trial, and to look for updates. See the privacy page.

What happens when the trial ends?

Sorting pauses. Preview and Undo keep working, and nothing you already sorted is touched. Enter a license key to continue.

Can it break my programs or projects?

Recursive sorting is off by default, and even when on, folders containing programs or code projects are skipped. You can always undo the last sort.

Does it work offline?

Yes. A license keeps working for 45 days between online checks, so occasional offline use is fine.

Can I move it to a new PC?

Yes. Use "Deactivate this computer" in the License tab, then activate on the new one.

Mac or Linux?

Windows only for now.
