Homesoft Sorter runs on your computer. It does not read, upload or analyse your files anywhere except on your own PC, and it has no analytics or advertising.

What stays on your PC

Your folders, rules, activity log and license are stored locally in %APPDATA%\com.homesoft.sorter. Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes them.

What the app sends over the internet

  • Free trial check. Once (and again if the trial data is missing), the app sends a salted SHA-256 hash of your Windows machine identifier to our trial server so the trial cannot be restarted by reinstalling. We store that hash and the date it first appeared. We cannot reverse it into your identifier. Like any web server, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) sees your IP address in routine request logs.
  • License activation and re-checks. Your license key and a computer name (for example "Homesoft Sorter on MY-PC") are sent to Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider and merchant of record, to activate and periodically re-check your license. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy applies to that data.
  • Update checks. The app asks GitHub for the latest version file. GitHub sees your IP address as it would for any download.

Payments

Purchases are handled entirely by Lemon Squeezy. We receive your name, email and country to deliver your license and handle support; we never see your card details.
